## Core Frameworks & Mental Models across the 8 Units

### 1. Hierarchical Reductionism & Systems Emergence (Unit 1: Chemistry of Life)
- **Principle**: Novel emergent properties arise at each level of biological organization (molecules $\to$ organelles $\to$ cells $\to$ tissues $\to$ organs $\to$ organisms $\to$ ecosystems).
- **Decision Rule**: Deconstruct biological phenomena to chemical fundamentals (electronegativity gradients, hydrophobic interactions, free energy), then reconstitute emergent dynamic feedback loops.

### 2. Chemiosmotic Proton-Motive Energy Coupling (Unit 2: The Cell)
- **Principle**: Catabolic electron flow down inner-membrane redox chains pumps protons ($H^+$) into enclosed compartments, driving rotational ATP synthesis via Mitchell chemiosmosis.
- **Decision Rule**: Distinguish substrate-level phosphorylation (direct enzymatic phosphate transfer) from oxidative/photophosphorylation (indirect chemiosmotic gradient coupling).

### 3. Central Dogma & Multi-Tier Genetic Regulation (Unit 3: Genetics)
- **Principle**: Genetic information directs phenotype via DNA transcription and mRNA translation, modulated by epigenetic chromatin remodeling, operons, and alternative splicing.
- **Decision Rule**: For Mendelian inheritance, separate independent gene assortment ($9:3:3:1$) from linked loci where recombinant frequency measures chromosomal distance in centimorgans (cM).

### 4. Population Genetic Equilibrium & Evolutionary Synthesis (Unit 4: Evolution)
- **Principle**: Evolution is the generational change in allele frequencies within a population gene pool. Natural selection is the only evolutionary mechanism producing adaptive divergence.
- **Decision Rule**: Use Hardy-Weinberg ($p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1$) as a statistical null baseline; deviations identify non-random mating, selection, genetic drift, or gene flow.

### 5. Cladistic Parsimony & Tree of Life Mapping (Unit 5: Diversity)
- **Principle**: Evolutionary relationships are inferred by shared derived characters (synapomorphies) forming monophyletic clades, adhering to the principle of maximum parsimony.
- **Decision Rule**: Distinguish homologous traits (shared ancestry) from analogous traits (convergent evolution); avoid paraphyletic groupings.

### 6. Transpiration-Cohesion-Tension & Vascular Bulk Flow (Unit 6: Plants)
- **Principle**: Long-distance xylem water transport operates under negative tension pulled by stomatal transpiration; phloem translocation operates under positive hydrostatic pressure from source to sink.
- **Decision Rule**: Water flows spontaneously from higher water potential to lower water potential ($\Psi = \Psi_s + \Psi_p$).

### 7. Homeostatic Negative Feedback & Countercurrent Physics (Unit 7: Animals)
- **Principle**: Internal fluid stability (ICF/ECF) is maintained by negative feedback loops; antiparallel countercurrent fluid flow preserves favorable diffusion gradients along entire anatomical surfaces.
- **Decision Rule**: Action potential propagation is governed by voltage-gated ion conductances; renal medullary gradients are established by the countercurrent multiplier of the loop of Henle.

### 8. Thermodynamic Energy Dissipation & Nutrient Biogeochemical Cycles (Unit 8: Ecology)
- **Principle**: Energy flows unidirectionally through trophic food webs and dissipates as metabolic heat (~10% efficiency); chemical nutrients cycle indefinitely between biotic and abiotic pools.
- **Decision Rule**: Logistic population growth is capped by environmental carrying capacity ($K$); habitat fragmentation accelerates the positive-feedback extinction vortex in small populations.
